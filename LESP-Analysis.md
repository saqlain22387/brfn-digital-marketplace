
# LESP Analysis – Compliance & Quality Assurance

## 1. Legal – GDPR

We must follow the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) when collecting and storing producers' personal information through our form. We should only collect the information needed for our system, keep it secure and make sure it is not shared with unauthorized people. This helps protect producers' privacy and ensures their personal information is handled safely and legally.

## 2. Ethical – Food Safety

We have an ethical responsibility to make sure that products listed as organic meet the correct standards. We should check organic claims using reliable evidence or certification before displaying them on our platform. This helps prevent misleading customers, supports better purchasing decisions and ensures that product information is accurate and trustworthy.

## 3. Social – Trust

Building customer trust is important for the success of our system. If a product is out of stock, we must clearly show that it is unavailable instead of making customers think they can purchase it. Keeping stock information accurate and up to date improves the customer experience, avoids disappointment and shows honesty and transparency.

## 4. Professional – Code of Conduct

Our group has a professional responsibility to work together respectfully and make sure everyone contributes fairly. When some team members were absent, we communicated with each other, shared responsibilities and focused on completing the project tasks. We tried to support each other and make sure that the absence of any group member did not affect our progress.
