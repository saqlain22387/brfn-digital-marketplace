LESP Analysis – Compliance & Quality Assurance
1. Legal – GDPR
2. We must comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) when collecting and storing producers personal information through our form. We should only collect the information necessary for our system, keep it secure, and ensure that it is not shared with unauthorized people. This helps protect producers' privacy and ensures that their personal data is handled responsibly and legally
 2. Ethical – Food Safety
 We have an ethical responsibility to ensure that products listed as organic meet the appropriate standards. We should verify organic claims using reliable evidence or certification before displaying them on our platform. This helps prevent misleading customers, supports informed purchasing decisions, and promotes food safety by ensuring that product information is accurate and trustworthy.
Social – Trust
Building customer trust is essential for the success of our system. If a product is out of stock, we must clearly display it as unavailable rather than allowing customers to believe it is available for purchase. Providing accurate and up-to-date stock information improves the customer experience, avoids disappointment, and demonstrates honesty and transparency.
Professional – Code of Conduct
Our group has a professional responsibility to work together respectfully and ensure that all members contribute fairly. When team members were absent, we followed the principles of our group code of conduct by communicating with each other, sharing responsibilities where appropriate, and focusing on completing the project tasks. We aimed to remain professional, support the team, and ensure that the absence of any member did not unnecessarily affect our progress.
