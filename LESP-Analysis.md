
# LESP Analysis – Compliance & Quality Assurance

## 1. Legal – GDPR

We must follow the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) when collecting and storing producers' personal information through our form. We should only collect the information needed for our system, keep it secure and make sure it is not shared with unauthorized people. This helps protect producers' privacy and ensures their personal information is handled safely and legally.

## 2. Ethical – Food Safety

We have an ethical responsibility to make sure that products listed as organic meet the correct standards. We should check organic claims using reliable evidence or certification before displaying them on our platform. This helps prevent misleading customers, supports better purchasing decisions and ensures that product information is accurate and trustworthy.

## 3. Social – Trust

Building customer trust is important for the success of our system. If a product is out of stock, we must clearly show that it is unavailable instead of making customers think they can purchase it. Keeping stock information accurate and up to date improves the customer experience, avoids disappointment and shows honesty and transparency.

## 4. Professional – Code of Conduct

Our group has a professional responsibility to work together respectfully and make sure everyone contributes fairly. When some team members were absent, we communicated with each other, shared responsibilities and focused on completing the project tasks. We tried to support each other and make sure that the absence of any group member did not affect our progress.

## 5. Quality Assurance
Quality Assurance means making sure that your system works correctly, provides accurate information and meets the expected requirements.
In our project, we are developing a platform involving producers, organic products and customers. We need to make sure that everything works properly before the system is used.
What should we include?
1. Functional Testing
Check that all features work correctly. For example:
- Producers can submit product information.
- Customers can view available products.
- Products can be displayed correctly.
- Users can submit information without errors.
2. Data Validation
Make sure users enter correct information into forms.
For example:
- Product names should not be empty.
- Prices should be entered in the correct format.
- Email addresses should be valid.
- Organic claims should have supporting evidence.
3. Security Testing
Check whether personal information is protected and whether unauthorized users can access restricted information.
4. User Acceptance Testing (UAT)
Ask users to test the platform and provide feedback. This helps identify problems that developers might have missed.
5. Continuous Improvement
Regularly review the system, fix errors and improve features based on feedback
## 6. Risk Management
Risk Management means identifying possible problems before they happen and deciding how to prevent or reduce their impact.
For example, imagine your platform displays organic products.
- A producer provides false organic certification?
- Customer information gets leaked?
- The system displays incorrect prices?
- A product is out of stock but still available for purchase?
- Your website stops working?
These are potential risks.
Your group needs to identify these risks and explain how to manage them.
What should you include?
1. Identify Risks
Find possible problems that could affect your platform.
2. Assess Risks
Understand how serious each problem could be and how likely it is to happen.
3. Risk Prevention
Introduce measures to reduce the possibility of problems.
4. Monitor Risks
Regularly check the system to identify new problems.
5. Contingency Planning
Prepare a backup plan in case something goes wrong
